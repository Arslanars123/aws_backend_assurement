10. DESCRIPTION OF THE CONTROL WORK

10.1 CONTROL PLAN (TENDER CONTROL PLAN OR THE COMPANY'S OWN)

A process review for a potential procurement control plan forms the basis for preparing the case's control plan, which provides an overview of controls and documentation for the contract.

If no supply control plan is submitted before the price offer, the company's own control plan will be used.

The control plan is submitted for customer approval at a project review meeting. If no meeting is held with construction management, the control plan is sent with the company's process review results for client approval.

10.2 QUALITY ASSURANCE OF THE PROJECT

Inspections are carried out by the project manager or another person specifically appointed as the inspector, who must be familiar with the company's quality assurance system.

10.3 CHECKING DOCUMENTS

Revised drawings, construction meeting minutes, and similar documents are sent to the company's e-mail.

It is assumed that all revised drawings include a revised subscription list and that revisions are clearly marked.

Quality assurance documents are presented at construction meetings.

10.4 INFORMATION FOR EMPLOYEES

Before work begins, craftsmen and apprentices are informed about the work, especially difficult processes.

Information regarding the project's quality and environmental requirements is also provided.

10.5 IN-DEPTH CONTROL

The control of materials upon arrival at the construction site.

Delivered goods are checked for correct quantity and quality according to the inspection plan.

Factors important to the environment, including the construction site layout, are part of this in-depth management.

Incoming control must be documented.

10.6 PROCESS CONTROL

Inspections specified in the control plan are carried out during the work.

Deviations and their rectification are performed in accordance with the agreement.

Environmental factors are included in the process control, which must also be documented.

10.7 FINAL INSPECTION

A responsible person conducts a final inspection when the work or parts of it are completed.

This final inspection is an internal activity, but evidence of it may be provided.

10.7 DOCUMENTATION

A copy of the quality assurance documentation will be sent to the client or its advisers by agreement.

All documents, including documentation of the agreed quality assurance, are stored for the current liability period.
